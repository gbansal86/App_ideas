# FreeMediaTools — Categorized Tool Catalog

**Research snapshot:** 2026-08-22  
**Reference:** https://freemediatools.com/

FreeMediaTools currently advertises **787+ free online tools**. This document captures the tools that could be independently verified from the live homepage and the site's indexed service lists/search-visible pages during research. The XML sitemap was not retrievable by the research tooling, so this should be treated as a **verified catalog snapshot**, not a claim that every one of the advertised 787+ URLs was captured.

The site contains many duplicate or near-duplicate tools under slightly different names; those are retained where useful because they reveal SEO/keyword strategy.

# Product Research Takeaways

## Strongest reusable categories for our app

1. PDF/document processing
2. Image compression/conversion/editing
3. Video editing/conversion
4. Audio editing/transcription
5. YouTube creator utilities
6. JSON/CSV/XML/YAML conversion
7. Developer utilities
8. SEO/network diagnostics
9. Text-processing utilities

## Categories to deprioritize initially

- Generic games
- One-off novelty generators
- COVID-era trackers
- Highly niche calculators
- Duplicate SEO pages that expose the same underlying functionality

## Important competitive insight

FreeMediaTools uses many **near-duplicate pages and alternate keyword names** for similar functionality. That likely helps it target long-tail SEO queries, but it can make the product feel fragmented. Our product should instead use a strong canonical tool with aliases/SEO landing pages that all resolve to one polished implementation.
